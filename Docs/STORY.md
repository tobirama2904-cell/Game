# AFTER THE SIGNAL — original narrative outline

**Genre:** survival road drama. **Setting:** Pacific Northwest, 2041. Ten years after the Silence, relay stations can still exchange brief messages when the atmospheric interference lifts. The infection is a neurological fungus spread through wounds and abandoned reservoirs, not a copy of an existing game pathogen. The journey is about deciding whether to broadcast a location when doing so might endanger the people already living there.

## Cast

* **Mara Reed, 32:** former maintenance engineer of the Oregon emergency radio network. She once switched off a relay to protect a shelter from raiders; that decision also left strangers without help. She isn't seeking forgiveness, only to make a choice she can defend.
* **Ethan Reed, 17:** Mara's brother, injured while repairing an antenna. He knows Mara changed the station logs. His illness is an infection of a wound, not supernatural immunity.
* **June Mercer, 48:** leader of Black Point, a former ferry terminal community. She rations medical supplies and knows the radio channel is compromised.
* **Cal Voss, 39:** salvage crew operator who intercepts distress calls. He believes controlling the airwaves is the only way to keep his crew alive.

## Full-game arc (design, not implemented)

### Act I — A voice through static
Mara and Ethan cross an Oregon logging road toward a relay tower. An operator's journal points them to a clinic; the medicine buys Ethan time. At the tower they hear Black Point answer and learn that another group heard the same transmission. The first playable episode in the current UE source covers only this three-step outline with text cues; it does **not** contain the following scenes.

### Act II — The cost of a name
Black Point needs an antenna part from Cal's occupied depot. Mara can barter, steal it quietly, or expose the settlement's location to secure passage. June finds the altered logs and asks Ethan whether he still trusts his sister. Their conflict is personal rather than a good/evil prompt.

### Act III — Open channel
The interference clears for twenty minutes. Mara can transmit Black Point's position to scattered survivors, transmit a decoy location to draw Cal away, or remain silent while June's sick residents wait for supplies. Each choice has a measurable cost: arrivals, raids, relationships and medicine. There is no perfect ending. The final image is Ethan listening beside an unlit receiver; what comes over it depends on what the player broadcast.

## Episode one: scene beats and sample dialogue

1. **Cold open, roadside:** Intermittent static. Ethan asks, “You think someone's still listening?” Mara answers, “Then we'd better give them something to hear.” Camera holds on a lamp blinking in the distance; no exposition dump.
2. **Operator's room:** A handwritten note: “The clinic still has two sealed packets. If someone brings a sick kid, let them have both. I don't need another broadcast as much as they need tomorrow.” The player discovers it by interacting, not by a cinematic taking control away.
3. **Clinic:** Ethan hesitates before opening the last packet. “What if someone else comes?” Mara: “Then I hope they find us.” This establishes the act-three decision without announcing it.
4. **Relay tower:** Mara keys the microphone: “If you hear me, we're still here.” Black Point answers through static: “We see your light.” Camera would stay on Mara's hand as she realizes that others might see it too.

**Implementation note:** currently only abbreviated text lines, pickups and mission stages are in the UE5 source. There are no implemented cutscenes, audio performances, choice system or endings yet. This document is a script outline for future production, not a description of completed gameplay.
